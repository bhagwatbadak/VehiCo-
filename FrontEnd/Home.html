Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How does AutoMob 24/7 help during road emergencies? expand_more
We connect you instantly with verified tow trucks, mechanics, and roadside helpers near your GPS location. Average response time is under 25 minutes. Humanity first — we reduce road threats and keep you safe.
Are service providers verified and reliable? expand_more
Yes. Every mechanic, tow driver, and garage partner goes through rigorous background checks, certification verification, and regular quality audits. Reliability & Secure is our core promise.
How do I register as a garage or tow service provider? expand_more
Click "Garage Login" or "Tow Provider" from the login section, then register your business. Our team reviews and approves your account within 24 hours. Grow your startup with AutoMob!
Is AutoMob available in my city? expand_more
We currently operate in 200+ cities across India with rapid expansion. Use the search bar to check service availability near your location in real time.
How is the pricing calculated? expand_more
Pricing is transparent and shown upfront before you confirm any booking. No hidden charges. You pay what you see — distance, service type, and time of day are factored in clearly.
About Us

AutoMob 24/7 — Born on the Road

We started with one question: "What if your car breaks down at midnight on a highway?" AutoMob 24/7 is the answer. We connect automobile owners with a trusted network of garages, tow providers, petrol pumps, and roadside heroes — all in one app.

Our mission: Reduce road threats, provide humanity's help, and make every driver feel safe and secure. We are building India's most caring automobile ecosystem — where happy customers, growing startups, and caring careers all thrive together.
